Arnika

The Brief

Rooted in nature, crafted for modern wellness.

Arnika is a herbal wellness brand inspired by the healing power of nature. Built around purity, balance, and traditional botanical wisdom, it offers natural oils and remedies for everyday well-being. The identity blends organic forms, earthy tones, and clean typography to create a brand that feels authentic, calming, and trustworthy—bringing nature's goodness into modern lifestyles.

Year 2024
Industry Cold-Pressed Oils
Tools Adobe Illustrator · Adobe Photoshop
§ 02 The Mark Logo & Theory
Logo Theory

A symbol of growth, balance, and nature in harmony.

The Arnika mark is inspired by botanical leaves arranged in a radial form, representing growth, vitality, and holistic wellness. Its clean geometric structure creates a timeless identity that feels both natural and premium.

Concept
Represents nature, healing, and balance. Form
Built using repeated leaf elements in a symmetrical layout. Behaviour
Simple, scalable, and recognizable across print and digital media.
