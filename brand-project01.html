Vestura

The Brief

An AI-powered fashion platform that lets people discover their style before they buy.

Vestura combines AI-powered virtual try-on with premium fashion, helping users discover the perfect fit before they buy. Its editorial identity blends timeless design with modern technology.

Year 2026
Industry Clothing Brand
Tools Adobe Illustrator · Adobe Photoshop
§ 02 The Mark Logo & Theory
Logo Theory

A mark designed to express individuality through timeless design.

Vestura's identity is built on the belief that fashion should be experienced before it's owned. Its custom letterforms blend bold geometry with soft curves, expressing the balance between individuality and precision. Designed to feel timeless and distinctive, the mark adapts effortlessly across digital experiences, apparel, packaging, and retail while remaining instantly recognizable.

Concept
A symbol of discovering your style before making it your own. Form
Bold geometric forms softened with organic curves for a modern luxury feel. Behaviour
Designed to work seamlessly as both the full wordmark and the standalone V icon.

§ 04 Process Sketch & Development

From rough mark to final form.

The Vestura identity began as a series of hand-drawn explorations focused on creating a wordmark that felt bold, timeless, and unmistakably unique. Through multiple refinements, each letter was carefully shaped to balance expressive curves with clean geometry, resulting in a custom identity that reflects confidence, individuality, and modern fashion.

§ 07 Packaging Applied Design
Packaging

Packaging that protects today and serves tomorrow.

Vestura's cylindrical packaging is designed to maximize space efficiency while protecting garments during delivery. Its reusable structure encourages a second life—as a planter reducing waste and extending the product experience. By combining sustainable materials with thoughtful design, the packaging becomes part of the brand story rather than something that's thrown away.

Built to last beyond the unboxing, every package reflects Vestura's vision of thoughtful design and conscious living.
