01 Featured Series

Mantra

This artwork merges sacred Sanskrit calligraphy, typographic illustration, and visual storytelling to form a portrait of Lord Shiva entirely through Devanagari script. Instead of traditional brush strokes or digital painting, carefully arranged Sanskrit characters follow the natural contours of the face, using variations in scale, spacing, rotation, and density to create depth, texture, and expression

The composition incorporates revered Shiva mantras such as "ॐ नमः शिवाय" (Om Namah Shivaya), "महादेव" (Mahadev), and verses inspired by the Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra, symbolizing transformation, meditation, and eternal consciousness.Every character contributes to the portrait, transforming language into a visual expression of spirituality, devotion, and timeless artistry.

02 Featured Series

Neon Eden

This hand-illustrated artwork explores the fusion of digital illustration, vibrant color theory, and surreal visual storytelling, depicting a tranquil figure immersed within a luminous forest ecosystem. Every element—from the intricate tattoos and botanical textures to the glowing mushrooms and layered foliage—was carefully illustrated to create a rich, immersive composition that balances realism with imaginative fantasy.

Created entirely by hand, the illustration showcases advanced digital artistry through the use of layer effects, gradient lighting, custom brushes, blending modes, texture overlays, and atmospheric color grading in Adobe Illustrator. The interplay of neon hues against the dark environment enhances depth and contrast, transforming the artwork into a vibrant celebration of creativity, nature, and contemporary digital illustration.

03 Featured Series

Mindscape

This conceptual illustration explores the relationship between human consciousness, history, architecture, and imagination through a layered visual narrative. Constructed entirely in Adobe Illustrator, the composition transforms the human mind into a symbolic landscape where monuments, cultures, landscapes, and geometric elements coexist to represent memory, knowledge, and creative thought. Every object has been carefully illustrated and arranged to create a balanced composition that encourages viewers to interpret their own meaning.

Using clean vector forms, intricate line work, layered compositions, and a bold color palette, the artwork showcases precision and attention to detail while demonstrating advanced illustration techniques. The interplay of architectural structures, surreal symbolism, and vibrant light beams creates depth and movement, resulting in a contemporary editorial illustration that celebrates storytelling through visual design.

04 Featured Series

Threads of Tradition

This illustration celebrates the richness of India's cultural heritage through a contemporary vector composition inspired by traditional folk art, temple architecture, and ornamental storytelling. Centered around a graceful female figure, the artwork is surrounded by symbolic elements including elephants, birds, floral motifs, and architectural details that reflect the country's diverse artistic traditions. Each component has been thoughtfully arranged to create a harmonious visual narrative that honors India's timeless craftsmanship.

Created entirely in Adobe Illustrator, the composition showcases precision through clean vector forms, layered symmetry, intricate detailing, and a vibrant color palette inspired by Indian festivals and classical art. By blending traditional symbolism with modern digital illustration techniques, the artwork reimagines cultural storytelling in a bold editorial style while demonstrating strong composition, color harmony, and visual balance.

Featured Publication

What are we?

What Are We? explores the uncertain space between friendship and love — the emotions that exist when two people care deeply for each other but struggle to define what they truly are. The story focuses on quiet moments, emotional hesitation, and the comfort found within unspoken connections.

The design was created to feel personal, youthful, and emotionally intimate. Bright yellow tones were used to represent warmth, nostalgia, and the comforting energy shared between the characters, while the handwritten typography adds a raw, diary-like quality to the publication. The layouts remain minimal and spacious to keep the focus on expressions, pauses, and emotional tension, allowing the visuals to communicate feelings that words often cannot express.
